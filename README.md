# ScrumPulse

**A free, offline-first Scrum workspace for facilitators who want structure without bureaucracy.**

ScrumPulse brings the complete Sprint workflow into one focused browser-based board. Plan real capacity, organize the Product and Sprint Backlogs, facilitate every Scrum event, and preserve the evidence needed to inspect and adapt—without creating an account or maintaining a server.

[Open ScrumPulse](https://treslines.github.io/app.html) · [English overview](https://treslines.github.io/) · [Visão geral em português](https://treslines.github.io/pt/)

## Sprint workflow

- **Daily Scrum:** Keep the event within 15 minutes with focused prompts, speaker timing, and visible participation.
- **Capacity and availability:** Account for vacations, public holidays, Scrum events, absences, and delivery overhead before selecting work.
- **Product and Sprint Planning:** Organize Epics, Features, Stories, Bugs, Tests, and Sub-tasks; define Product and Sprint Goals; record dependencies; and lock the commitment baseline.
- **Sprint Review:** Use practical guidance and a timebox to inspect the outcome with stakeholders and adapt the Product Backlog.
- **Sprint Retrospective:** Capture what went well, what did not, and accountable improvement actions with owners and due dates.

## Insights and shared standards

- **Roadmap:** Forecast the Product Goal against estimates, dependencies, team availability, delivery capacity, and its target deadline.
- **Burndown:** Compare the Sprint commitment, scope changes, and remaining work. Velocity is planning evidence—not an individual performance metric.
- **Sprint History and Stats:** Preserve read-only Sprint snapshots and review recent delivery and ceremony trends.
- **Vacation:** Plan team absences and public holidays once, then reuse them automatically in Sprint capacity.
- **Knowledge Base and Decision Log:** Keep useful references and important decisions close to the work.
- **Scrum Search, Ownership, DoD, and DoR:** Consult offline Scrum guidance and make responsibilities and shared quality standards explicit.

## Local-first by design

The ScrumPulse application stores its workspace in the browser and has no runtime dependency on an account, hosted database, or synchronization server. Export and import JSON backups to protect the workspace or continue it on another device. The product tour, GitHub links, and external ticket links require an internet connection.

ScrumPulse is designed as a facilitator-focused workspace rather than a live multiuser issue tracker. Share the facilitator's screen during a session, or transfer a JSON backup when another person needs to continue the workspace.

## Getting started

1. Configure the team, Sprint length, dates, and working hours in Board Settings.
2. Record vacations, public holidays, and availability to calculate effective capacity.
3. Define the Product Goal and deadline, then prepare and order the Product Backlog.
4. Move ready Stories, Bugs, or Tests into the Sprint Backlog, add a Sprint Goal, and give commitment.
5. Facilitate the Sprint and inspect Burndown, Roadmap, Stats, and Sprint History as evidence for the next adaptation.
